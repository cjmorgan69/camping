Weather: Open-Meteo forecast (BOM ACCESS-G and other models blended), Adelaide time. Campgrounds and coastline: © OpenStreetMap contributors, plus a short hand-made list of well-known sites. WikiCamps has no public data feed, so check site details, fees and access in WikiCamps before you go.

Offshore means the wind is blowing from the land out to sea at that campground. Coast direction is the way the shoreline faces, worked out from the coastline within about a kilometre of the site; bays, headlands and dunes can still shelter or funnel the wind.
